Questions

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Reelstuber cuts, captions and exports your video inside your browser. We only save your edits (clip times, styles, the post text and captions if you make them) so you can come back later. Signed out, even those stay in your browser.

Can I paste a YouTube link?

No: YouTube doesn't let other sites download its videos. Use your own copy instead: in YouTube Studio go to Content, open the ⋮ menu next to the video and choose Download. Only use videos you have the rights to.

Is it free? Is there a watermark?

It's free while we're growing, and there's no watermark. Your handle only shows if you add it.

Which browsers work?

Editing works in any modern browser. Exporting works best in Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer. Each reel plays through once while it records, so a 30-second reel takes about 30 seconds.

How do the captions work?

Three ways: write them on your device (speech-to-text runs in your browser; the first time it downloads a small model), import an .srt or .vtt file, or type them in. You can edit every word.

Do I need an account?

No. Try everything without one: your projects stay in this browser. Sign up to keep them in your account, save a brand kit and share templates.
